About Couchlog

Couchlog is a personal app to keep track of the movies and series I watch.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

The dates of upcoming episodes also come from TVmaze, whose data is licensed under CC BY-SA.

Privacy. Couchlog stores what you add: your watch history, ratings and notes, your time zone and your email. Daily backups are kept in private storage at Cloudflare R2. When you open Couchlog from outside my home, the connection goes through Cloudflare, which could technically see what passes through. If you want your data removed, tell me.

Welcome to Couchlog

Couchlog keeps track of the movies and series you watch. Here is how it works, in eight steps. The red numbers in each picture show where to press.

1. Find a title

  1. Search (at the top of every page): type the name of a movie or series.
  2. The + on a poster adds it to your To Watch list straight away.
  3. The name or the poster opens its page.

2. Keep it for later

  1. Add to To Watch puts it on your To Watch list. Movies and series each have their own. Press it again (Remove from To Watch) to take it off.

3. A movie's page

  1. Rate it or add a note: give it stars and write a note for yourself.
  2. The day you watched it. It is today unless you change it.
  3. Mark as watched saves the watch with its date. After the first time it says Watch again, for each time you see it again.
  4. Date unknown, when you don't remember the day.
  5. Mark as not watched takes a watch back. With several watches, each has its own Remove this watch.

At the bottom of the page, Where to watch lists the streaming services that have it in your country.

4. A series' page

  1. Stop watching moves the series to Dropped, with its history kept. There the button is Bring back, next to Remove from all lists, which you can undo for a few seconds. Before you start a series, this is where Add to To Watch is. The bar above shows how many episodes you've watched.
  1. A season: press it to open or fold its episodes.
  2. Mark Season 1 as watched marks every episode that has aired. Once they all are, it becomes Mark Season 1 as not watched, and Watch Season 1 again appears for a rewatch.
  3. The round button marks one episode as watched and turns green. If you skipped earlier episodes, Couchlog asks whether you watched them too. Pressing a green one again counts another watch.
  4. The − takes back the latest watch of that episode.

5. Your lists

  1. The tabs. Series: Watching (the series you've started), To Watch (the ones you want to start), Finished (watched to the end) and Dropped (the ones you stopped). Movies: To Watch and History (every movie you've watched, with its date).
  2. A name or a poster opens its page.

6. Home

  1. News: what comes out in the next 7 days from your lists. A banner opens its page.
  2. Up Next: the next episode of each series you're watching. The name opens the series.
  3. The round button marks that episode as watched, and the card moves on to the next one.

7. Calendar

The Calendar shows what comes out further ahead, day by day: new episodes of the series you're watching, and the cinema and streaming dates of everything on your To Watch lists.

  1. A name opens its page.

8. Profile

Time spent watching adds up every watch: movies, series and the total, rewatches too.

  1. Your time zone, so “today” and the release days are your own.
  2. Save time zone after choosing a new one.
  3. Log out.

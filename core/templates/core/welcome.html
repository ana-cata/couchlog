Welcome to Couchlog

Couchlog keeps track of the movies and series you watch. Here is how it works, in five steps.

1. Find a title

Press Search at the top and type the name of a movie or series. Its page shows what it's about and where you can stream it.

2. Keep it for later

Press Add to To Watch on its page. Movies and series each have their own To Watch list.

3. Mark what you watch

On a movie's page, pick the day and press Mark as watched. On a series' page, press the round button next to an episode, or mark a whole season at once. Every watch is kept with its date, so you can watch something again, and anything you mark can be taken back.

4. The Series tabs

  • Watching: the series you've started.
  • To Watch: the series you want to start.
  • Finished: the series you've watched to the end.
  • Dropped: the series you stopped watching. Their history is kept, and you can bring them back any time.

5. Home and the Calendar

Home shows what comes out in the next 7 days, and Up Next: the next episode of each series you're watching, ready to check off. The Calendar shows the release dates of everything on your lists, further ahead.
